    How to play

    Each day there's a category and a hidden answer word. A spectrum appears, like tiny ↔ huge. Guess any word in the category: it's marked on the spectrum by where it sits compared with the answer.

    Each guess reveals the next spectrum. After four, you make a final guess with all four showing. Five guesses in all; an exact hit ends the game straight away.

    The answer is the centre dot and every line passes through it, shifted so the answer sits where it truly belongs on that spectrum. A guess's distance from the dot is how far off it was, and the side it's on says which end word it was closer to. The chart turns so the newest line is upright; the arrow keys or a click on a line turn it back.
