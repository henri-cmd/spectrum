        How to play

        Six rounds a day on one board. The board's two axes are fixed for the day; every round has its own category, and you have 30 seconds.

        Rounds 1 to 3: a target appears. Type a word from the category and press Enter; it lands where it truly sits on both axes. Round 4: the target is the centre of the scope. Round 5 is reversed: you are given a word and tap where you think it sits. Round 6 is the boss: as many words as you can at one target before the clock runs out.

        A hit scores its ring, 50 for the bull then 25, 10 and 5, plus a point for every second left on the clock. No answer scores nothing. Your day's total is the sum of the six rounds.
